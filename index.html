Employment & Accommodation Questionnaire

For Filipino applicants interested in working in the Czech Republic. Please answer the following questions.

1. Full name
2. How long have you been living in the Czech Republic?
3. How much longer would you like to stay in the Czech Republic?

Accommodation information

First 6 months: Accommodation paid by the company.
After 6 months: Proposed employee contribution of 5,000 CZK per month.
Shared house: With other Filipino employees.
Bedroom: Up to 3 people per room.
Bathroom and kitchen: Shared with other residents on the same floor.
4. Are you willing to pay 5,000 CZK per month for accommodation after the first 6 months?
5. Are you willing to live in a shared house with other Filipino employees?
6. Are you comfortable sharing a bedroom with 2 other people (3 people in one room)?
7. Are you comfortable sharing a bathroom and kitchen with other residents on the same floor?
